Because our products are food items, we cannot accept returns of opened jars or for a change of mind.

If your order arrives damaged, leaking, with the wrong product or with something missing, tell us within 48 hours of delivery with a photo or video of the package. We will send a free replacement or give you a full refund for that item, whichever you prefer.
