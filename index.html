Our Story

From our kitchen in Kikkar Khera to yours

Ghar Masala started the way most good things in Punjab do: in a family kitchen. For years we made our own masale at home because the packets from the market never tasted the same. Friends and neighbours started asking for a jar, then another.

Today we still make every batch the same way. Spices grown without chemicals, dried under the Punjab sun, ground by hand and packed fresh. If we wouldn't cook with it at home, it doesn't go in the jar.

Good to know

How do I place an order?

Choose your spice and size, tap Add to Cart, then Checkout. Fill in your delivery address and pay securely online by UPI, card, net banking or wallet through PayU.

Do you deliver across India?

Yes. We ship across India. Shipping is a flat ₹60 per order, and FREE on orders of ₹499 and above.

Is there any colour or preservative added?

No. Our Red Chilli Powder is 100% red chilli and our Turmeric Powder is 100% turmeric. Nothing else goes in.

How should I store the spices?

Keep the jar tightly closed in a cool, dry place away from sunlight. Use a dry spoon. Red Chilli lasts 12 months and Turmeric 18 months from packing.
